1. Build Your Prompt Using All Five Building Blocks

P

Persona

Tell Copilot who to act as so it adopts the right pedagogical tone and expertise.

"Act as a Scottish P6 primary teacher..."
A

Aim

State clearly what you want created, including CfE Experiences & Outcomes.

"Create a 45-min lesson plan on fractions (MNU 2-07a)..."
R

Recipients

Specify who the resource is for, including stage, reading age, or ASN scaffolds.

"For a mixed-ability S2 class with dyslexic-friendly text..."
T

Theme

Set the style, context, or tone (e.g., active, inquiry-led, Scottish context).

"Use an encouraging tone with local Scottish examples..."
S

Structure

Define the exact output format so it is ready to copy into Docs, Word, or Teams.

"Format as a 4-column table with an exit pass at the end."
Weak Prompt (Too Vague)

"Make a worksheet about Scottish renewable energy."

Result: Generic US-centric text, wrong reading level, requires heavy editing.

Strong PARTS Prompt (Classroom Ready)

[P] Act as an experienced Scottish secondary geography teacher. [A] Create a 50-minute lesson plan on offshore wind energy in the North Sea, aligned to CfE Third Level (SOC 3-08a). [R] Design for a mixed-ability S2 class with a vocabulary support box for literacy barriers. [T] Keep the tone active and inquiry-led. [S] Format as a table with Timing, Teacher Action, Pupil Activity, and a 3-question exit pass.

2. Ready-to-Copy Classroom Templates (Click Copy & Swap Bracketed Text)

Primary / BGE

Success Criteria Rubric

Act as a Scottish primary teacher. Create a 4-point success criteria rubric for a [P6 persuasive writing piece on plastic waste] aligned to CfE [Second Level LIT 2-24a]. Use encouraging, child-friendly "I can..." statements and format as a clean table.

Secondary / SQA

Retrieval Practice Starter

Act as an SQA [National 5 Biology] teacher. Write an 8-question retrieval practice starter quiz on [cell structure and transport]. Include 4 knowledge recall questions and 4 problem-solving questions for S4 pupils, followed by a concise marking key.

ASN & Feedback

Two Stars & a Wish Feedback

Act as a supportive Scottish teacher. Review this anonymised pupil paragraph against these criteria: [1. Clear opening, 2. Descriptive verbs]. Give Two Stars and One Wish in encouraging language without rewriting their work: [Paste anonymised text]

Pro Tip: Follow up with "Now simplify this to a reading age of 9" or "Turn this into 10 flashcards" for Learning Activities.
